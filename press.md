# Apoyu press kit

**The one-liner.** The recovery app that reads your night entirely on your phone, tells you the truth
about it, and hands you a little fire creature's take on your day. No hardware, no subscription, no
number if you don't want one.

## Fact sheet

- **Name:** Apoyu (the creature is Apoyü, a fire axolotl)
- **What it is:** a recovery companion for iPhone and Apple Watch. Every morning it computes a
  recovery score on the device and deals a collectible archetype card voiced by the creature
- **Price:** free. No subscription. One optional one-time purchase at launch (the Keeper's Flame,
  $7.99, cosmetic, launch window only)
- **The pledge:** nothing you had ever goes behind glass. The score, the daily card, the dare, the
  briefing, the collection, and hide-score are free permanently
- **Privacy:** raw biometrics are processed on device and never uploaded; only derived daily results
  sync. Details: [apoyu.app/trust](/trust)
- **Platform:** iOS 26, watchOS companion with complication and widgets. Built native, with a
  hand-animated Rive reveal and a Liquid Glass design
- **Developer:** [Name], solo developer of record
- **Availability:** [launch date] on the App Store
- **Press contact:** [press@apoyu.app]
- **Promo codes:** available on request. No embargoes; first-look access by arrangement

## Three true angles

1. **The architecture:** on-device biometric processing as a working consumer product. What leaves
   the phone is a number, not your night.
2. **The character:** recovery reframed from dashboard to companion. The daily card is spoiler-safe
   by design; people share the joke, never the number.
3. **The craft:** a solo developer shipping a native iOS 26 app with a hand-animated creature, a
   watch app, and a no-subscription business model.

**What Apoyu is not** (please quote us on this): not a medical device, not a diagnosis, not an AI
coach, and not trying to replace anyone's hardware. The category word we use is companion.

## Quotes

Developer of record: *"I wanted a recovery app I could recommend to an anxious friend. That meant the
raw data stays on the phone, the number can be hidden, and the creature never guilts you."*
