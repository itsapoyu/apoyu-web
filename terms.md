## 5. What happens to what you earned when membership is not active

This is our promise about the things you collect.

### 5.1 Nothing you earned is removed

If you decide not to start a trial after your preview morning, or your trial or membership ends for any reason, your account moves to **Archive Mode**. In Archive Mode you keep, and can keep using:

- every collectible card you earned, and your Collection
- your past Calendar and receipts
- Card Detail for every card you own
- sharing of cards you already own
- Settings, your account, and all privacy and data controls (including data requests and account deletion)
- membership management, so you can start or restart membership at any time

### 5.2 What pauses

In Archive Mode, Apoyü does not make anything new for a new day: no new recovery reading, collectible, Daily Call, brief, Today's Nudge, or daily look back at your history. Starting or restarting membership resumes new mornings from that point on. Mornings that passed while you were in Archive Mode are not created after the fact.

### 5.3 What membership never changes

Your membership state never changes the quality of the recovery algorithm, the quality of any health or safety reading, rarity odds, which archetypes you can get, or the meaning of the Comfort word, a card's Origin, or the front of any collectible. A member and a non-member on the same morning are scored the same way.
