# Apoyu Support

For questions, bug reports, account help, or anything else about the app, email **support@apoyu.app**.

Typical response time is 1–2 business days.

For account deletion, use the in-app affordance: **Settings → Account → Delete Account**. This revokes your Apple refresh token (if you signed in with Apple) and erases all user-linked data within seconds.

For privacy questions specifically, see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and the [Health Data Privacy notice](/health-privacy) (Washington residents).
