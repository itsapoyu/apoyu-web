# Apoyu Support

For questions, bug reports, account help, or anything else about the app, email **support@apoyu.app**.

Typical response time is 1 to 2 business days.

## What do I need to use Apoyü?

An iPhone with Apple Health. An Apple Watch (or another device that writes heart rate variability, resting heart rate, and sleep to Apple Health) is what makes the readings yours: Apoyü reads your overnight data through Apple Health and scores it on your phone.

You do not need a Watch to start. If Apple Health has nothing to read yet, Apoyü still builds your full first morning on day one from typical starting values, and tells you the reading is low confidence. Once a wearable is recording your nights, each morning is scored on your own data.

## Account deletion

Use the in-app option: **Settings → Account → Delete Account**. This revokes your Apple refresh token (if you signed in with Apple) and erases all user-linked data within seconds.

For privacy questions specifically, see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and the [Health Data Privacy notice](/health-privacy) (Washington residents).
