Contact

Email: support@apoyu.app

Apoyü is built and run by one person, and every message is read by that person. You will normally hear back within 2 business days. Data and privacy requests are always answered within the windows the law requires; see Privacy and Health Data Privacy.

When reporting a problem, it helps to include your iPhone and Apple Watch models, your iOS and watchOS versions, and a screenshot if something looks wrong. Please never email health data; we do not need it and do not want it.

Common questions

What do I need to use Apoyü?
An iPhone and an Apple Watch worn to sleep. Apoyü reads the night your Watch records through Apple Health and scores it on your phone. Other wearables and sleep apps that write to Apple Health are not used. Without Apple Watch data there is nothing to read, so the app tells you it needs a Watch rather than guess, and no card is made.

Does it work with Garmin?
Honestly, not well, and we would rather tell you at the door. Garmin does not share overnight HRV with Apple Health, so Apoyü would be scoring on incomplete data. If your training watch is a Garmin, Apoyü is not the right tool yet.

Why are my scores lower than my old tracker's?
Because Apoyü scores you against your own recent baseline instead of a population curve. A normal night reads as the middle, not as an 85, so the scale has somewhere to go on your genuinely great mornings. It usually takes a couple of weeks to feel calibrated, and the app tells you when its confidence is still low. If numbers stress you, Settings has a switch to hide them entirely; the comfort word carries the meaning.

Where does my health data go?
Your individual readings never leave your phone. The score is computed on the device; only per-day results (the score and daily summary values) sync to your private account, and the AI that phrases the daily coaching receives derived values only, never raw readings. The full accounting is at apoyu.app/health-privacy, and it is written to be checked, not believed.

Is Apoyü medical advice?
No. Apoyü is a general wellness app, not a medical device. Scores, briefs, and the Daily Call are informational guidance computed from your own data. They are not diagnosis or treatment, and they are no substitute for a professional. If something feels medically wrong, talk to a doctor, not an app.

How do I fix missing or wrong data?
First check iOS Settings, Health, Data Access and Devices, Apoyu, and make sure the categories are on. Then check that your watch actually recorded the night (it needs to be worn during sleep and charged). If a specific morning still looks wrong, email us with the date. The app also lowers its own confidence when data is thin, so a cautious score after a patchy night is the system working.

How do notifications work?
All optional. Daily notes are scheduled on your phone. If you have not opened the app for a few days, we may send at most two short notes, then nothing more until you are back. The app learns your typical wake time so the morning note arrives when you are actually up. Turn notifications off in Settings, Notifications, or at the iOS level.

What does Apoyü cost?
Apoyü Membership is $49.99 a year or $7.99 a month in the US (the App Store shows the price in your currency, and the price on the purchase sheet is the one that applies). Either plan can start with a 14-day free trial, once per person. Before any of that, every new account gets one complete free morning, with no commitment, so you can see the whole thing first. There is no permanent free plan.

What happens if I do not subscribe, or my membership ends?
Your account goes into Archive Mode. You keep everything you already earned: every card, your Collection, your Calendar and receipts, Card Detail, sharing, Settings, and all your privacy and data controls. What pauses is new mornings: no new reading, card, Daily Call, brief, or nudge until you start or restart membership. Nothing you earned is ever taken away.

How do I cancel?
iOS Settings, tap your name, Subscriptions, Apoyu. To avoid being charged when a free trial ends, cancel at least 24 hours before it ends. You keep full access until the end of the trial or the period you paid for.

How do refunds work?
Membership is billed by Apple, so refunds go through Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com, or your App Store purchase history. We cannot issue App Store refunds ourselves.

My membership is not showing.
Open Apoyü and tap Restore Purchases on the membership screen, signed in with the same Apple ID you subscribed with.

What is Founding Keeper?
A small cosmetic mark (an alternate card back, a little badge, and optional share decoration) that an account earns once, at its first paid membership payment after the free trial, during a founding window we will announce. It is not something you can buy. Starting a trial does not earn it, and cancelling does not take it away.

Deleting your data

Everything is deletable, in the app, without emailing anyone, whether or not you are a member:

  1. Open Apoyü and go to Settings, Account, Delete Account.
  2. Confirm. The app revokes your Sign in with Apple authorization, deletes your records from our servers, clears the data stored on your phone, and signs you out.

Deletion is immediate in live systems and cannot be undone; your collection and history are gone. Routine backups overwrite within 7 days. Two things survive, neither of them health data: an anonymized record that a deletion happened, and your released share code (held 90 days so it cannot be instantly reassigned).

Two notes:

  • Deleting your account does not cancel your App Store subscription or trial. Cancel it first (iOS Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Apoyu).
  • Your Apple Health data is untouched. Apoyü never had your raw readings; they remain in Apple Health, under your control, as always.

If you cannot access the app (lost device, cannot sign in), email support@apoyu.app with the subject "Delete my account" from the email associated with your account and we will complete it for you after verification.

To exercise any other privacy right (access, correction, portability, or rights under the Washington My Health My Data Act, CCPA, or GDPR), email support@apoyu.app; the Privacy Policy and Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy list each right and how we handle it.
